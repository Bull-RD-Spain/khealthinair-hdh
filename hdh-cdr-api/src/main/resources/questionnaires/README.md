# Questionnaires definition in HL7 FHIR R5

The following are the questionnaires used in the project. They are defined in HL7 FHIR R5 format to be imported prior the ingestion of the responses data.

- Part A. Socio-demographic data

- Part B. Time Spent In The Scenario/Location

- Part C. ICHOM (International Consortium for Health Outcomes Measurement), that in turn is composed, in this approach, by the following
  - SACQ (Self-Administered Comorbidity Questionnaire)
  - PROMIS Global-10 Questionnaire (Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement Information System)
  - WHODAS-12 (The World Health Organization Disability Assessment Schedule 2.0)
  - WHO-5 (The World Health Organization-Five Well-Being Index)

- Part D. PREMs (Patient-Reported Experience Measures, adaptation Of The Paris Survey)
  - PaRIS (Patient-Reported Indicator Surveys)
  - Observational checklist (for IPCHEM)
  - FS/FOT (Forced Spirometry/Forced Oscillation Technique) - FS/FOT V6M
  - CAT (COPD Assessment Test)  
  - mMRC (modified Medical Research Council Dyspnea scale)
  - TAI-12 (Test of Adherence to Inhalers 12)
  - ATC (Asthma Control Test)
  - MiniAQLQ (Mini Asthma Quality of Life Questionnaire)
  - SNOT-22 (Sino-Nasal Outcome Test)

- Part E. PART E.Quality Of Life
  - EQ-5D-5L (5-level EQ-5D Questionnaire)
  - EQ-5D-Y-5L (5-level EQ-5D Youth Questionnaire)

- PART F. Mental Health
  - PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire)
